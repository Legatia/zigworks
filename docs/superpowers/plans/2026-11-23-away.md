# harness: away and travel, the door (sections 1 and 2 of the household spec) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The house knows when nobody is in it and acts like it: away mode (on after everyone has been out `[butler] away_after = 12h`, during a trip, or `/away until DATE`; off at `first_home` or `/away off`) asks once to turn the heating down, switches the lights the owner picked on and off in the evening at varied minutes, makes any door, window or motion urgent, notes the doorbell, says one line at 20:00 and warms the house before the return. Trips (`state/trips/`, `/trips`) come from the calendar and from travel mail: a check-in line 24 h before a flight with the airline's own link, the diary's leave-by for the airport, the evening before the weather and what to pack, flight status only from a source the owner set, times in the place's own zone. homemcp gains doorbells that push `ring`; a `doorbell` routine says "Someone's at the front door." (with "probably the DHL parcel" when one is due), urgent while away, with [Unlock the door] only for a lock home.ini lets unlock and only as the owner's own one-tap request.

**Architecture:** Pure modules `away.zig` (state file, on/off rules, the lights' plan, notes, the summary's words) and `trips.zig` (legs and trips from calendar events and mail, merging, files, reminder keys, packing list, airline table). `core.zig` gets two new sections (`---- away ----`, `---- trips ----`) and one-line hooks (open/close, tick, resourceUpdated, commitPoll, command, diaryPost). `triggers.zig` gets `while = away|home`. `diary.zig` gets the airport margin for flights. Routines in `examples/butler` (`away.wf`, `doorbell.wf`, `trips.wf`, triggers, rules) offered by `/setup routines` through `butler.zig`. homemcp: kind `doorbell` in model, config, events (a ring is a pulse), discover, sim.

**Tech Stack:** Zig 0.17.1, std only; macOS, Linux, Windows.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-harness-butler-household-design.md` sections 1, 2 and Tests; `2026-11-23-harness-butler-design.md` (UX rules, red lines); `2026-11-23-harness-butler-duties-design.md` (urgent, escalation, the diary's leave-by); `2026-11-23-harness-email-design.md` (travel mail, deliveries).

## Global Constraints

- Three other builders work at the same time (errands + upkeep, health + habits + the evening, carcloud's Tesla backend): core.zig and butler.zig edits stay in new sections and one-line hooks; new files where possible.
- Every new Core field set in `Core.open`; state files written whole through `util.writeReplace`; secrets never in logs or output; members and speech never approve anything guarded.
- Messages follow the UX rules: plain, short, units, no tool names or JSON; one tap; read back; quiet by default (quiet hours hold what isn't urgent).
- Red lines: no camera; nothing unlocked, opened or disarmed unattended; away mode acts only on non-guarded devices.
- Baseline: harness 1032 pass, 20 skipped, smoke 103 (131 with HOMEMCP_BIN, CARCLOUD_BIN, CALMCP_BIN); homemcp its unit tests and smoke.
- Tests from `harness/`: `zig build test --summary all --cache-dir $SCRATCH/zc-away > FILE 2>&1`; `-Dtest-filter=NAME` while iterating; Debug and ReleaseSafe; with the stand-ins; cross builds (x86_64-windows, x86_64-linux-musl, aarch64-linux-musl; `zig build` and `unit-build`) with `-p` into a scratch folder. Templates read the same with `\r\n`; the smoke script runs under macOS's `/bin/sh`. Fake clocks for times.

## Review Focus

### Red lines and guarded devices

1. **Away mode never acts on a guarded device.** Its unattended calls are `home_set` only, as `schedule:away`, each passing the gate; the lights are those `rules` lets `schedule:away` switch on (exact lines setup writes, lights of home.ini that are not guarded); `/away lights` refuses anything but a light that isn't guarded; homemcp refuses guarded devices on `home_set`. (`away_lights_are_only_unguarded_lights`, `lived_in_lights_go_on_and_off_at_varied_minutes`.)
2. **Nothing unlocks unattended.** [Unlock the door] is an `approve` with `yes` before a fixed `home_guarded` unlock (a money tool: asked every time; the tap is that call's Allow once); no answer in 10 minutes is nothing; `fallback` on it is refused when read (existing test); members get [I'm on it] only; while away there is no unlock button. (`the_doorbell_unlocks_only_on_the_owners_tap`, `no_unlock_button_without_a_lock_that_unlocks`, `while_away_the_doorbell_is_urgent_without_unlock`.)
3. **No camera.** Doorbells are their button only; discover still leaves cameras out (existing test extended with a doorbell camera).
4. **Events are data.** A doorbell's `hint` is a carrier's name from a fixed list or "a parcel", never mail text; travel mail reaches the model only as data; a check-in link is shown only on the airline's own domain. (`the_parcel_hint_names_only_a_known_carrier`, `a_check_in_link_off_the_airlines_domain_is_not_shown`.)
5. **Flight status claims nothing without a source.** No `[butler] flight_status`: no status line at all. (`flight_status_only_from_a_source`.)
