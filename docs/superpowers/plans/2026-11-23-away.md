# harness: away and travel, the door (sections 1 and 2 of the household spec) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The house knows when nobody is in it and acts like it: away mode (on after everyone has been out `[butler] away_after = 12h`, during a trip, or `/away until DATE`; off at `first_home` or `/away off`) asks once to turn the heating down, switches the lights the owner picked on and off in the evening at varied minutes, makes any door, window or motion urgent, notes the doorbell, says one line at 20:00 and warms the house before the return. Trips (`state/trips/`, `/trips`) come from the calendar and from travel mail: a check-in line 24 h before a flight with the airline's own link, the diary's leave-by for the airport, the evening before the weather and what to pack, flight status only from a source the owner set, times in the place's own zone. homemcp gains doorbells that push `ring`; a `doorbell` routine says "Someone's at the front door." (with "probably the DHL parcel" when one is due), urgent while away, with [Unlock the door] only for a lock home.ini lets unlock and only as the owner's own one-tap request.

**Architecture:** Pure modules `away.zig` (state file, on/off rules, the lights' plan, notes, the summary's words) and `trips.zig` (legs and trips from calendar events and mail, merging, files, reminder keys, packing list, airline table). `core.zig` gets two new sections (`---- away ----`, `---- trips ----`) and one-line hooks (open/close, tick, resourceUpdated, commitPoll, command, diaryPost). `triggers.zig` gets `while = away|home`. `diary.zig` gets the airport margin for flights. Routines in `examples/butler` (`away.wf`, `doorbell.wf`, `trips.wf`, triggers, rules) offered by `/setup routines` through `butler.zig`. homemcp: kind `doorbell` in model, config, events (a ring is a pulse), discover, sim.

**Tech Stack:** Zig 0.17.1, std only; macOS, Linux, Windows.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-harness-butler-household-design.md` sections 1, 2 and Tests; `2026-11-23-harness-butler-design.md` (UX rules, red lines); `2026-11-23-harness-butler-duties-design.md` (urgent, escalation, the diary's leave-by); `2026-11-23-harness-email-design.md` (travel mail, deliveries).

## Global Constraints

- Messages follow the UX rules: plain, short, units, no tool names or JSON; one tap; read back; quiet by default (quiet hours hold what isn't urgent).

## Review Focus

### Red lines and guarded devices

1. **Away mode never acts on a guarded device.** Its unattended calls are `home_set` only, as `schedule:away`, each passing the gate; the lights are those `rules` lets `schedule:away` switch on (exact lines setup writes, lights of home.ini that are not guarded); `/away lights` refuses anything but a light that isn't guarded; homemcp refuses guarded devices on `home_set`. (`away_lights_are_only_unguarded_lights`, `lived_in_lights_go_on_and_off_at_varied_minutes`.)
2. **Nothing unlocks unattended.** [Unlock the door] is an `approve` with `yes` before a fixed `home_guarded` unlock (a money tool: asked every time; the tap is that call's Allow once); no answer in 10 minutes is nothing; `fallback` on it is refused when read (existing test); members get [I'm on it] only; while away there is no unlock button. (`the_doorbell_unlocks_only_on_the_owners_tap`, `no_unlock_button_without_a_lock_that_unlocks`, `while_away_the_doorbell_is_urgent_without_unlock`.)
3. **No camera.** Doorbells are their button only; discover still leaves cameras out (existing test extended with a doorbell camera).
4. **Events are data.** A doorbell's `hint` is a carrier's name from a fixed list or "a parcel", never mail text; travel mail reaches the model only as data; a check-in link is shown only on the airline's own domain. (`the_parcel_hint_names_only_a_known_carrier`, `a_check_in_link_off_the_airlines_domain_is_not_shown`.)
5. **Flight status claims nothing without a source.** No `[butler] flight_status`: no status line at all. (`flight_status_only_from_a_source`.)

## Rulings

(Each: what – why – cost if wrong. Added to as they are made.)

1. **A doorbell's ring is a press.** What: homemcp kind `doorbell`; its main state is `ring` (pressed) or `idle`; every press heard as it happens is pushed, though it reads as the last (its `at` differs); a ring heard while connecting is kept, not pushed. Home Assistant: a doorbell `event` entity (its state is the press's time), a `binary_sensor` (on while pressed); Zigbee2MQTT: each state message with an `action` but `release`. Why: a doorbell has no lasting state, and a ring found after a restart is minutes old. Cost if wrong: a Zigbee2MQTT button whose every report repeats its last action would ring again (Limits says so).
2. **Discover finds doorbells by class or name.** What: an `event` of class doorbell, an `event` or `binary_sensor` named doorbell, ding or dzwonek, a Zigbee2MQTT button (an `action` expose) named so; no allow line (one is refused). A doorbell's camera is a camera: left out. Cost if wrong: a doorbell named otherwise is left out ("not a kind homemcp knows"), added by hand.
3. **`while = away|home` on triggers.** What: an event trigger with `while` counts its events only while away mode is on (or off); in the other state an event is dropped as it comes (logged "dropped"), never kept for later. Why: a door opening while you're home is not news later. Cost if wrong: an event in the minute away mode turns on may be missed (it counts from the next one).
4. **The parcel hint.** What: a doorbell's event gets the field `hint` from the deliveries due today in the owner's mailbox (mailmcp's `mail_list`, as the trigger's own call through the gate): ", probably the DHL parcel" for the first carrier of a fixed list (DHL, DPD, UPS, InPost, …), ", probably a parcel" for one not on it, "" for none. Nothing else of a mail is said or logged (sizes only). Why: mail is data from outside; a sender's name could say anything. Cost if wrong: a shop's own courier reads "a parcel".
5. **`tag = no` on a trigger's `say`.** What: the line without `[NAME]` in front; for the butler's routine triggers (away's doors, the doorbell while away). Why: routine posts are plain (UX rule 1), as `tag no` does for workflows. Cost if wrong: none found.
6. **Away mode is the Away routine.** What: on, off and its work only while `workflows/away.wf` is read (`/setup routines` offers it); without it `/away` says so. Why: no surprises: doors made urgent and lights switched only once the owner turned it on. Cost if wrong: one more tap at setup.
7. **On and off.** What: on when everyone has been out `[butler] away_after` (12h; from homemcp's `everyone_left` time, else from when harness first saw everyone out), when a trip is under way and nobody is home (or nobody is known to be), or by `/away on|until DAY [HH:MM]`; off at a `first_home` after it came on (or any time someone is home, when it came on because all were out), `/away off`, and, where the home file has no people, when the trip's or the given return has come. After `/away off` it comes on by itself only for a later leaving or trip. Why: the spec, and a trip of one of two must not make every door urgent for the one at home. Cost if wrong: a household whose `[people]` misses someone gets away mode while that one is home (the README says to list everyone).
8. **The start: one message, asked once.** What: harness starts `away.wf` as `schedule:away` (the owner's `/away` as `owner`) with the line ("Away mode is on: everyone has been out since 08:41. Doors, windows and movement come to you at once, and the living room light goes on and off in the evening."); when the thermostat is set above `[butler] away_temp` (17), the same message asks [Heating to 17 °C] [Leave it] (12 h); once per time away; held in quiet hours like any routine's question; withdrawn when away mode ends. `schedule:away` and `schedule:trips` are harness's own schedules: a workflow may name them without a triggers.ini section. Cost if wrong: a 01:00 away-on asks at 07:00.
9. **The lights.** What: home.ini's lights that aren't guarded and go on and off, of them those `rules` lets `schedule:away` switch on (exact lines setup writes: two by default, a living room's first; `/away lights NAME, …|none` writes them again and refuses anything else); on from 19:00 and off from 22:30, each up to 30 minutes later, a minute per light and per evening from a seed made as away mode comes on; each tried once an evening; only lights it switched on are switched off; nothing said of them; away mode ending leaves them as they are. Why: lived in, not a timer anyone can learn. Cost if wrong: lights on an hour before a winter sunset or after a summer one.
10. **Away's own calls.** What: `home_set` alone, as `schedule:away`, through the gate with nobody to ask (a rule must allow each call; ask counts as no), logged as gate and tool lines; `home_guarded` is refused in code whatever a rule says. Why: the red lines. Cost if wrong: none found.
11. **Doors, windows and movement while away.** What: a trigger per door or window sensor (`to is open`) and per motion sensor (`to is someone there`), `while = away`, `urgent = yes` (through quiet hours, escalated as `[butler] escalate` says), a plain `say` ("The back door opened while you're away."), and with a speaker `announce` ("The back door opened. The owner has been told."); each noted for the 20:00 line. Cost if wrong: a cat sitter sets them off (the README: `/away off` while someone stays).
12. **The 20:00 line.** What: once a day at 20:00 (the owner's time) while away: "All quiet at home." when no door, window or movement came since the last one, else "At home today: the back door opened at 11:02."; then each ring ("The front door bell rang at 14:10, probably the DHL parcel.") and parcel ("A parcel was left at 14:10.", from `Core.awayParcelLeft`, for the deliveries' "delivered"); sent without a sound in quiet hours (as the diary's lines); none the day away mode came on after 20:00 (what came meanwhile goes in the next). Cost if wrong: none found.
13. **The heating back.** What: two hours before the return (a trip's last arrival plus `[butler] travel`, or `/away until`'s day at 18:00 unless a time is given), the thermostat to `[butler] home_temp` (21), once, with a line ("The heating is going back to 21 °C for your return at about 18:00."); also when away mode goes off, unless the first one home has the Arriving routine to do it; only when it is set at the away temperature or below (one you left higher, or one that can't be read, stays). Why: the spec; never raise heating the owner chose. Cost if wrong: a house set to 18 while away stays at 18.
14. **Away's work runs on a thread of its own**, every 20 seconds at most (at once when homemcp pushes who is home), outside the background slots runs take; `home://presence` is read every 10 minutes and on each push. Cost if wrong: a light up to 20 seconds late.
