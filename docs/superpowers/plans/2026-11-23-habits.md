# harness: health reminders, habits, the evening and the butler preset (household spec §5, §6, §8) — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Three duties of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-harness-butler-household-design.md`. **Health (§5):** `/med add NAME at 08:00, 20:00` sets reminders with [Taken]; not taken after 30 minutes, once more; with `/med tell NAME MEMBER` (opt-in per medication) that member is told after an hour; `/meds` lists them; nothing beyond the schedule and "taken" is kept. `[butler] bedtime = 23:30` (opt-in, `/bedtime`): the owner's first message of the night after it gets one gentle line ("It's past half eleven."). No medical advice. **Habits (§6):** once a week harness reads what the owner asked the home to do (the log); the same non-guarded `home_set` call within 20 minutes on at least 4 of the last 14 days becomes one suggestion a week, "You switch the coffee machine on at about 6:45 on weekdays. Shall I do that?" [Yes] [No, and don't ask again]; Yes shows, then writes, a routine. **The evening (§8):** at `[butler] evening` (opt-in, offered by setup at 21:00 or before quiet hours), one message: tomorrow's first event and when to leave, the weather, the car's charge, bills due tomorrow, what waits in the inbox, each line only when it has something. The `butler` definition preset the setup offers: courteous, brief, discreet; how to address the owner asked once.

**Architecture:** three new files hold everything that is not wiring, each pure but for its state file: `meds.zig` (the schedule and `/med`'s grammar, `state/meds`, what is due at a minute, the texts, bedtime's night and words), `habits.zig` (the log read for the owner's own `home_set` calls, the pattern, the question's words, the routine's three files, `state/habits`), `evening.zig` (the lines from what was read, the car's and the bills' readings, tomorrow's first event, the weather's prompt, `state/evening`, the `butler` preset's file). `core.zig` gets five namespaces at its end (`Meds`, `Bedtime`, `Habits`, `Evening`, `Manner`) with their tests, and one-line hooks: Core fields (set in `Core.open`), `tick`, a button prefix `m`, a reply to a reminder, `/med` `/meds` `/bedtime` `/evening` and `/setup butler`, the command line's log withholding `/med`'s words, `setupAnswer`'s dispatch for items `habit …`, `evening`, `manner`, and setup's offers. `config.zig`: `[butler] bedtime` and `evening` (HH:MM or off). `butler.zig` untouched.

**Tech Stack:** Zig 0.17.1, std only; macOS, Linux, Windows.

**Spec:** household spec §5, §6, §8 and Tests; the butler spec's UX rules and red lines; the duties spec (escalation to members, the diary's agenda and leave-by, people); the email spec (bills); the ledger (the log of runs and tool calls, routines and `/setup routines`, agent definitions, quiet hours, members, the inbox's one-tap items).

## Review Focus

### Never acting unasked

1. **Habits suggest; they never act.** A pass writes nothing but `state/habits` and one item; the routine's files are written only on the owner's Yes, shown first (`a_habit_is_suggested_and_written_only_on_yes`).
2. **Never a guarded device.** A call counts only for a device home.ini has, that is not guarded (lock, alarm, valve, `guarded = yes`), not a switch setup couldn't place, not a switch named a valve, and that allows the action; checked again on Yes, which refuses if it changed (`habits_never_suggest_guarded_devices`, `yes_refuses_a_device_guarded_since`).
3. **Only the owner's own requests.** Tool lines of runs whose first line says `by: owner` and whose argument is readable (a private run keeps only sizes); members' (`member:`), the house's speech (`room:`), schedules', triggers', workflows' (a routine's own calls among them) never count (`habits_count_only_the_owners_own_requests`).
4. **The routine may do that one call alone**: its rules line is the exact canonical call for its one step (`[workflow:habit-…/act]`), started by a schedule at the time and days shown.
5. **One suggestion a week, and a No is for good** (`one_suggestion_a_week_and_a_no_is_remembered`).
6. **The evening only reads**: `car_status`, `mail_list` and `search`, each through the gate as `schedule:evening` and logged; nothing is asked; a read the gate doesn't allow leaves its line out (`the_evening_reads_through_the_gate_and_asks_nothing`).
7. **The preset changes nothing until the owner answers**; it changes main's definition only (`the_butler_preset_asks_how_to_address_you_once`).

### Health data kept to the minimum

1. **`state/meds`** holds the schedule (name, times, who is told) and the reminders of today and yesterday: their minute, whether taken, the messages they went in, a token; older ones are dropped. No history of taking (`meds_keep_the_schedule_and_taken_only`).
2. **The log never names a medication**: `/med`'s words are withheld from the command line ("add (withheld)"); reminders are `med` lines with the time and the event only (`the_log_never_names_a_medication`).
3. **A member reads the time, never the name**: "The owner hasn't marked the 08:00 medicine as taken; the reminder went an hour ago." (`a_member_is_told_after_an_hour_when_asked`).
4. **No advice**: every health line is a fixed text; the model writes none; the `butler` preset says no medical advice.
5. **Members and speech** can't use `/med`, `/meds`, `/bedtime` (owner commands as today); reminders go to main only.

## Rulings

(Each: what – why – cost if wrong.)

1. **Reminders ring at their minute, through quiet hours and busy events.** – The owner set that minute; a reminder held for a digest, or silent at 23:00, would be missed. – A sound at night the owner asked for.
2. **No medication name in the log.** `/med`'s words are logged as its first word and "(withheld)"; reminders, presses and the member's line are `med` lines (kind `chat`) with the time and the event. – Health data kept to the minimum; the log is kept forever. – The log can't say which medicine a line was about.
3. **`state/meds` keeps today's and yesterday's reminders only**, then drops them. – "Nothing beyond the schedule and taken". – No "did I take it yesterday?".
4. **Only the owner's tap, or reply, marks taken.** A reply to a reminder saying taken, took it, done, yes or ok (any case, trailing punctuation) marks it; any other reply is an ordinary message. – One tap, a text reply works too. – "I took the pill" in other words is a message, not a mark.
5. **A reminder not sent within 3 hours of its minute is skipped** (harness was down). – A 08:00 pill at 13:00 is no reminder. – A missed reminder after a long outage.
6. **The member: once, an hour after the first reminder**, in their own chat with the bot, without a sound in quiet hours; the owner is told who was; a tap afterwards tells the member it was taken. A member who can't be reached is said to the owner. – No surprises on either side. – One more line each.
7. **Bedtime's night** runs from bedtime to the end of quiet hours (06:00 without them); the line names the bedtime ("It's past half eleven.", "It's past eleven.", "It's past 23:40."), once a night (kept in `state/bedtime`), on the owner's first text or voice message in their own chats; commands and taps don't count. `/bedtime 23:30|off` writes config.ini. – Gentle, once. – An urgent message at 23:31 gets it too.
8. **Habits count successful `home_set` calls** (any server's `home_set`, homemcp's ordinary-device tool) with device, action and value the same; the device by home.ini's name or alias. – home_set is what "asked the home to do" is; `home_guarded` never. – Another tool's habit isn't seen.
9. **The window**: 20 minutes, not across midnight; at least 4 distinct days of the last 14; the time is the middle of the window's calls rounded to 5 minutes; the days are weekdays when every day was Mon–Fri, weekends when every one was Sat or Sun, else every day. – The spec's numbers; plain words. – A Mon/Wed/Fri habit runs every weekday.
10. **The weekly pass** runs from 10:00 the owner's time, 7 days after the last; its item is a schedule's (`schedule:habits`), so quiet hours and busy events hold it; it expires in 7 days; an unanswered one may come again after 28 days; No is never asked again; Yes is never suggested again. – At most one a week, and never nagging. – A declined habit can only be set by hand later.
11. **The habit's routine is silent when the device confirms**; a line when it doesn't. – Quiet by default; the owner asked for exactly this. – No daily read-back (`/workflow habit-…` shows each run).
12. **The evening is computed by harness**, not the model, but for the weather's line: search in the morning summary's town, then one memory-model call with the results as data ("none" when they don't say). – Each line only when it has something, deterministic. – A model call an evening.
13. **The evening's lines:** tomorrow's first timed event (not all day, cancelled or declined) with "leave by" at its start less `[butler] travel` when it has a place; the weather; the car from `car_status` ("Car 34 %, 150 km, plugged in."); bills due tomorrow from the owner's mailbox (`mail_list` with `due_in_days` 1) when it is on; "N things wait in /inbox" for the open items. Nothing at all: no message. – The evening is about tomorrow. – A bill due today isn't said (the bill routine said it 3 days before).
14. **The evening is opt-in**: offered by `/setup routines` after the routines (and `/evening HH:MM`), at 21:00, or 30 minutes before quiet hours when they start before 21:30; inside quiet hours it goes without a sound; during a busy event it waits for its end as a schedule's post; more than 2 hours late it is skipped for the day; once a day (`state/evening`). – No new daily message nobody asked for. – None found.
15. **The butler preset is `agents/butler.md`**, shown then written on the owner's answer to one question (a reply with how to address them, or Plainly), and main switched to it (`/agent main` goes back); topics keep theirs; `/setup butler` offers it alone. An answer that isn't a plain name (1–40 letters, spaces, `.`, `'`, `-`) leaves the question open. – The address is asked once, in the question itself. – Topics answer as main until `/agent butler` there.
