base-head.bin

The desk avatar's base head: the head every portrait is fitted onto. It is
made from ICT-FaceKit (USC Institute for Creative Technologies'
morphable face model, light version), https://github.com/USC-ICT/ICT-FaceKit,
at commit da5f95a607f5e6b37755b38d3385d7f2853732e5.

Files used, from FaceXModel/: generic_neutral_mesh.obj and the expression
shapes browDown_L/R, browInnerUp_L/R, browOuterUp_L/R, cheekPuff_L/R,
cheekSquint_L/R, eyeBlink_L/R, eyeLookDown_L/R, eyeLookIn_L/R,
eyeLookOut_L/R, eyeLookUp_L/R, eyeSquint_L/R, eyeWide_L/R, jawForward,
jawLeft, jawOpen, jawRight, mouthClose, mouthDimple_L/R, mouthFrown_L/R,
mouthFunnel, mouthLeft, mouthLowerDown_L/R, mouthPress_L/R, mouthPucker,
mouthRight, mouthRollLower, mouthRollUpper, mouthShrugLower,
mouthShrugUpper, mouthSmile_L/R, mouthStretch_L/R, mouthUpperUp_L/R,
noseSneer_L/R. Each is checked by SHA-256 in tools/make-base-head.mjs.

How it was made: node tools/make-base-head.mjs DATA desk-assets/base-head.bin
(tools/README). It keeps the face, the back of the head and the mouth's
inside (not the neck, nor the folds behind the eyelids), simplifies them to
4,500 vertices without moving any vertex, maps the expressions onto the 52
ARKit names (browInnerUp and cheekPuff are the sums of ICT's left and right
halves; tongueOut, which ICT doesn't have, is zero), adds the teeth, gums
and tongue with their jaw motion, and stores MediaPipe's 478 face landmarks
found on a render of the neutral head. Changes from the original: the
geometry was cut and simplified and the shapes recombined as said; the
upper teeth and upper gums were moved down 2 mm; the eyeBlink_L and
eyeBlink_R shapes were extended so the upper lid closes 0.3 mm past the
lower one.
