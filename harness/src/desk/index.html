          Your own avatar
          The fit files aren't installed.
          • They fit a photo onto the 3D head: MediaPipe's face landmarker (about 23 MB) and the base head.
          • harness's release archive has them in desk-assets/ beside the harness binary; harness finds them there too.
          • Built from source: sh tools/fetch-desk-assets.sh with this folder, and desk-assets/base-head.bin from the repository copied into it.

          They go in desk-assets/

          Upload a photo
          • The photo is fitted onto a 3D head and becomes the whole scene: its background, hair, shoulders and lighting.
          • These are refused, with the reason: a face turned more than about 8°, an open mouth or teeth showing, closed eyes, more than one face, a face too small to find.
          • These fit but may look off: glasses (the eyes move behind frames drawn on the skin), hair over the eyes, cartoon or anime styles (which may not be found at all).
          • Every avatar gets the same teeth and mouth, and turns its head a few degrees at most.

          • Facing the camera, eyes open, mouth closed.
          • One face, head and shoulders, wider than tall.
          • Works best without glasses.
          • JPEG or PNG, up to 10 MB.

          The photo stays on this computer, in avatars/NAME/.

          Add an eyes-closed version
          • Make one with an image tool: ask it to close the eyes and change nothing else. It's one image edit, not a video.
          • It must line up with the first photo, and the desk checks this.
          • Without it, closed eyes are drawn in the skin's tone.

          Recommended: the avatar rests with its eyes closed.

          Speak replies
          • New replies in the conversation you have open are read aloud once. A long one reads its first two sentences.
          • Urgent alerts are always read while the desk is open and not muted; in quiet hours, only they are.
          • A provider's voice is fetched by harness, so its key stays in harness.
          • Model and Voice belong to the chosen provider. Left empty, its default (in grey) is used. Choosing a provider of another kind clears them.
          • Providers and their keys are added in config.ini and with harness secret add (README: Speech).

          On Gemini's free tier, Google may use the spoken text to improve its products. A key on a paid plan keeps it out.
